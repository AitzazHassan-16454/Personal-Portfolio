How I work

Three commitments that have survived every project so far.

Ship early

Something clickable in week one beats a complete spec in month three. We iterate on the real thing, not a deck.

Working prototype by day 5
01 · in practice

A slice of the real thing, week one

Day 1 you get the core flow, clickable. Day 5 a build on a URL your team can open. Every decision after that is made against something you can use — not something you can only describe.

No decks, no wireframe graveyards

Measure everything

If it isn't instrumented it isn't shipped. Budgets, traces and regressions get caught in review, not in production.

Perf budget on every PR
02 · in practice

Budgets that argue back

p75 LCP, error budget and deploy frequency land in CI before the feature does. When a budget breaks, the PR says so — nobody has to open a dashboard to find out what happened last night.

A broken budget fails the build

Leave it better

Docs, tests, and a codebase the next person can actually move in. No heroic knowledge locked in my head.

Zero tribal knowledge
03 · in practice

Handover is part of done

An ADR for the trade-offs, tests around the sharp edges, a README the next engineer can trust. The last week of an engagement is spent making myself unnecessary, not indispensable.

The codebase is the documentation

How an engagement unfolds

No surprises, no discovery phase that never ends.

01

Discovery

Two weeks. We map the constraint that's actually blocking you and agree a measurable target.

02

Prototype

One risky path end to end in week one. If the approach holds, we build around it.

03

Build

Two-week increments, demo every Friday, perf budget enforced in CI from day one.

04

Hand off

Docs, runbooks, and a pairing week. You own it outright — no lock-in, no retainer.
