ContentForge
AI Content & Publishing

Enterprise content generation suffered from high wait times and disconnected audio synthesis. I architected a unified platform with Vector RAG, sub-200ms real-time SSE streaming, and client-side binary WAV header stitching across 9 distinct AI voices.

01 — The brief Why traditional blocking web requests fail content teams.

Content creators and publishers required high-speed, factually grounded article drafts along with synchronized multi-character narration. Legacy AI web tools forced users to wait 15 to 30 seconds for an entire generation to finish before rendering anything. If a generation failed or drifted off-topic, the entire round-trip was wasted.

Furthermore, voice generation required switching to external third-party tools, generating audio snippets for individual paragraphs, and manually assembling them in audio editors. Enterprises also needed strict workspace boundaries and token budget limits.

The brief was to engineer an end-to-end publishing studio: sub-second token streaming via Server-Sent Events (SSE), Vector RAG context retrieval, continuous 9-voice WAV audio generation stitched right in the browser, and enterprise Spatie RBAC.

02 — What I built Vector RAG engine, live SSE token pipeline, binary WAV stitcher, and Spatie RBAC.

ContentForge combines a robust Laravel 13 backend with a reactive Vue 3 and Inertia.js frontend, providing authors with an instantaneous, collaborative writing studio.

  • Vector RAG Engine — Ingests enterprise documents into high-dimensional vector embeddings. Semantic search retrieves high-relevance chunks to ground LLM generation and eliminate hallucinations.
  • Server-Sent Events (SSE) Streaming — Live token-by-token streaming from the Laravel pipeline directly into Vue 3 reactive editors, reducing time-to-first-token to under 200 milliseconds.
  • 9-Voice WAV TTS Stitching — Dynamic binary audio concatenation that merges multi-character dialogue snippets into a continuous single WAV audio file without browser stutter.
  • Spatie RBAC Multi-Tenancy — Granular role management, team permissions, audit trails, and per-workspace token allocation quotas.

The UI combines dark editorial ergonomics with live typing feedback, audio waveforms, and instant one-click exports to markdown, HTML, and audio podcasts.

03 — The hard part Binary WAV header assembly, resilient SSE pipes, and context pruning.

Binary WAV header stitching in real time

Synthesizing dialogue across 9 distinct AI voices produces separate PCM audio chunks. Decoding and re-encoding audio in the browser induces CPU spikes and introduces audible clicks at slice boundaries. I implemented a low-level binary parser that strips individual RIFF/WAV headers, calculates accurate byte lengths, and rewrites a single canonical 44-byte WAV header over the concatenated PCM stream on the fly.

Resilient long-lived SSE connections

PHP-FPM and Nginx typically buffer output before flushing to the client. I configured dedicated non-buffering SSE endpoints with explicit buffer flushing and heartbeat frames. Vue 3 handles client-side connection drops with automatic reconnection and draft resumption, so authors never lose content during intermittent connectivity.

Context thresholding and token efficiency

Feeding raw vector search matches into LLM prompts quickly inflates token costs and slows generation. I created an intelligent context pruning layer that ranks similarity scores, strips boilerplate, and constructs dense, compact system contexts.
